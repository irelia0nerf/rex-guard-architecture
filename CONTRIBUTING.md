# Feedback and contribution policy

This is a curated architecture portfolio. It is not currently accepting
unsolicited source-code patches, substantial documentation submissions, or
contributions to a product implementation.

## Public feedback

Non-confidential questions, incorrect references, and narrowly scoped factual
corrections may be reported through an issue. Identify the affected section and
explain the concern using public sources where possible.

Do not submit customer information, credentials, proprietary implementation
details, unpublished inventions, personal data, or material you are not entitled
to disclose. Read [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) before reporting sensitive concerns.

## Pull requests

Please obtain maintainer direction before opening a pull request. An unsolicited
pull request may be closed without review or merge.

This policy is not a contributor license agreement and does not request an
assignment of intellectual-property rights. Acknowledgment, discussion, or
review does not establish a transfer of title or a separate commercial license.
Any accepted substantial contribution requires its own appropriate rights and
permission review before incorporation.

## Reuse

Repository visibility and participation do not provide a general reuse license.
See [LICENSE](LICENSE). To request permission, open a non-confidential issue
requesting a contact channel; do not attach private material.
